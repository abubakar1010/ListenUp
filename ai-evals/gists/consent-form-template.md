> copy. Keep the signed form **outside** the repository.

**Project:** ListenUp, an English listening-practice web app.
**Purpose:** to check that our automatic gist feedback agrees with English teachers.
**Who runs it:** [owner name and contact for questions and withdrawal].

## What you will do

You will listen to [number] short recordings once each and type, in your own words, what
you understood. This takes about [minutes] minutes. It is not a test of you, and spelling
and grammar mistakes will not count against anything.

## What we will keep and who sees it

- The **text you typed**. The published project files contain that text, the teachers'
  grades and a case number such as `g17`, and nothing else about you: no name, email,
  voice or account.
- A teacher will read it and give it a grade. Teachers do not know who you are.
- The typed text, with the grades, will be stored in a **public** project repository, so
  anyone can read it. Before it is published we will remove any detail that could identify
  you (a name, place, employer or school) and **show you the final text. It is published
  only if you agree.**
- Your **signed form (which has your name) and the link between you and your number** are
  kept privately by [owner name], are never published, and are deleted on [date, for
  example when the project ends].
- The text may be used to test AI services that grade gists. It will **not** be used to
  train AI models, and we will only send it to services that agree not to train on it.

## Your choices

- Taking part is **voluntary**. You may stop at any time without giving a reason.
- You may ask to withdraw later by contacting [contact]. We will remove your text from
  the project's current files. Because the repository is public and keeps its history, and
  copies may already exist, we **cannot guarantee** that earlier copies disappear.
  [Owner: delete or change this sentence only if you can give a different promise.]
- Choosing not to take part does not affect any ListenUp account or service.
