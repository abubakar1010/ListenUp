# Coverage checklist and collection targets

Targets guide recruitment; they are not measurements or new quality gates.
`templates/collection-plan.csv` contains 50 **uncollected original-response** slots,
not responses. Allow more recruitment to fill observed gaps; never manufacture
or select grades to make a quota appear satisfied.

| Area | Proposed target | Evidence needed before completion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Passages | 10, about 5 original gists each | Teacher-approved audio, transcript and 3–6 frozen points; actual counts by passage |
| Original human gists | About 50, plus transformations | Verified private permissions and source records, public anonymous cases |
| Learner bands | B1 17 / B2 17 / C1 16 initially | Actual broad-band counts; report unknown without guessing |
| Levels | Initial 13 Needs work / 13 Fair / 12 Good / 12 Excellent | Actual adjudicated grades; all four levels present |
| AT-10 | At least 5, main idea and most supporting points | Actual Good/Excellent teacher grades with point coverage |
| AT-11 | At least 5 genuinely human-authored unrelated responses | Needs work; sidecar feedback names every missed point |
| AT-12 | At least 5 original/corrected pairs, across passages and bands | Teacher-verified same meaning, spelling/grammar-only changes, same level; discuss mismatches |
| AT-13 | At least 5 injection/control pairs | Actual human adversarial responses containing the demand; teachers ignore it and grade normally |
| Other meaning cases | At least 5 partial coverage and 5 unsupported/contradicted-claim cases (overlap allowed) | Covered/missed labels and specific wrong-claim evidence |
| Form boundaries | Record short/empty, over-limit or Unicode cases if supplied | Explicit runner-only/out-of-product tags; no synthetic substitutes in human set |
| Score boundaries | Seek natural near-50/70/90 cases | Preserve actual dimensions; report rounding disagreements, do not engineer them |
| Teacher overlap | Prefer all; at least 20 independently double rated | Actual n and subset composition; agreement before adjudication |
| AT-15 handoff | Same cleared cases available for future three runs | No consistency claim here; #109 evaluates primary/fallback and range ≤10 |
| Privacy/license | Every released record | Two-person review, source attribution, private consent/identity ledger |

The 50-slot plan allocates five original injection tasks, five unrelated tasks,
five grammar-error originals and five partial-coverage cases as recruitment
intent. Corrected/control partners are **additional**, for about 60 released cases
if all ten extra partners are retained. Count originals and transformations
separately; two texts from one participant are not two independent learners.
Adversarial cases are elicited stress cases, not evidence of spontaneous frequency.
Natural spelling errors cannot be guaranteed; recruit more or disclose a gap.
Targets overlap and must be verified after rating; neither a `target_level` nor a
condition tag proves that a case meets an acceptance test.

All ten selected source readers are distinct corpus speaker IDs; they are not
an accent-balanced sample. Books, narrative speech and historical vocabulary are
not representative of all everyday listening. Teachers may replace unsuitable
selections with equivalently licensed sources and version the references before
collection. Record dialect/genre gaps honestly; do not infer demographics from
corpus metadata. For B1 learners, preview difficulty without revealing the actual
passage, permit skipping and use teacher-approved replacements if needed.
