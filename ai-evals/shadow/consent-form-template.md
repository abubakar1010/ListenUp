> biometric data. The owner must review it, and have it checked for the speaker's country,
> before use. Replace every `[bracket]`. Give the speaker a copy. Keep the signed form
> **outside** the repository, in private, team-only storage.

**Project:** ListenUp, an English listening-practice web app.
**Purpose:** to check that our automatic speaking feedback agrees with English teachers and
is fair to speakers with different accents.
**Who runs it:** [owner name and contact for questions and withdrawal].

## What you will do

You will listen to [number] short recordings (60 to 90 seconds) and repeat each along with
the speaker, about [minutes] minutes in all, using headphones. You will be recorded. This is
not a test of you: we are testing our software.

## What we will keep and who hears it

- **Your voice recordings** and the **grades and word marks** a teacher gives them.
- Your **first language or region** (you choose a coarse option below), used only to check
  that our software is fair across accents. Not your name, age, email or location.
- **Teachers** will listen to the recordings. They do not know your name.
- Recordings are stored in **private storage that only [named team members] can open**.
  They are **never** published, put in a public repository, or sent to any service that
  keeps or trains on them. Only the grades, word marks and a speaker code (such as `S12`)
  are written in the public project files.
- If we test AI services with your recordings, we use only services that agree **not to train
  on them** or run models on our own servers.
- Recordings are **not** used to train any AI model.
- Your **signed form and the link between you and your code** are kept privately by
  [owner name] and never published.
- We will **delete your recordings** on [date, for example when the evaluation work is
  finished, and no later than [N] months from today], or sooner if you ask. Grades and word
  marks that do not identify you may be kept. [Owner: confirm this retention.]

## Your choices

- Taking part is **voluntary**. You may stop at any time without giving a reason.
- You may withdraw later by contacting [contact]. We will delete your recordings, and your
  grades and marks from the project's current files. Because the repository is public and
  keeps its history, we cannot guarantee that earlier copies of grades disappear; the audio
  itself is never in it. [Owner: change this sentence only if you can give a different promise.]
- Choosing not to take part does not affect any ListenUp account or service.
