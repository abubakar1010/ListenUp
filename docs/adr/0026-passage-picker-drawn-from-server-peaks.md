# ADR 0026: The passage picker draws the server's peaks itself, with two slider handles

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-03
- Source: issue #42; [SRS FR-CI-4, C2, UI-4](https://claude.ai/code/artifact/fd47a9cb-6515-4fde-a232-3ab8cc14f0dd); [Software Architecture 2 (wavesurfer.js)](https://claude.ai/code/artifact/6a30920f-b6a0-4131-a486-f2044c71bb13); final UI screen B04 and the design system's `lu-wave` component; decisions D2 and D15

## Context

The start-a-plan page asked for the passage as typed start and end times (ADR 0023). Issue #42 replaces that with a waveform: drag a start and an end handle, check the cuts by ear, see the length, and keep the typed times as an equal alternative (WCAG 2.5.7). Every passage is 30 s to 15 min and inside the clip (C2, D2, D15). The conversion job already writes the peaks, ten values a second on a 0 to 100 scale (ADR 0022), so the browser never decodes audio. The Software Architecture names wavesurfer.js for waveforms and segment picking. Screen B04 specifies the behaviour: handles are sliders (arrow keys 1 s, Shift + arrow 5 s, Home and End to the limits), typed times clamp with a message so the selection is always valid, "Hear start" and "Hear end" play 1.5 s either side of the cut and no more (UX-11: the first full listen stays fresh for Blind), and the length is announced a second after the learner stops.

## Decision

- **Draw the peaks ourselves; do not use wavesurfer.js here.** The picker is an SVG of bars (one path, the loudest peak per 3 px, recoloured inside the selection with a clip path) under two `role="slider"` handles, in the start-a-plan page's lazy chunk. wavesurfer.js 7.12 with its regions plugin would add about 17 KB gzip to that chunk (11.9 KB core, 4.9 KB regions), while the whole picker adds 3.5 KB (the chunk went from 3.1 to 6.5 KB gzip; the initial JavaScript is unchanged at 98.7 KB). More importantly, its regions are pointer-only: the plugin has no focusable handles, key handling or ARIA at all, so we would have had to hide them from assistive technology and build the accessible handles anyway. It would also own a media element and decoding paths this screen does not need. wavesurfer.js stays the Architecture's choice for later screens that need a playing waveform with a cursor (Transcript, Shadow); this ADR decides only the passage picker.
- **The selection logic is pure functions** in `src/features/session/passage.ts`, with the state (whole clip or a part, drafts of typed times, errors, the last adjustment note) in a reducer in `passageState.ts`. Components only render and dispatch.
- **Handles stop at the limits.** A handle moves between `end − 15 min` and `end − 30 s` (start) or `start + 30 s` and `min(clip end, start + 15 min)` (end); the other handle never moves while one is dragged or keyed. Each slider's `aria-valuemin` and `aria-valuemax` are those live limits, in seconds, with `aria-valuetext` such as "2 minutes 10 seconds".
- **Keys:** arrows move 1 s and Shift + arrows 5 s (B04); Page Up and Page Down also move 5 s, the slider pattern's large step; Home and End go to the handle's limits. A step goes to the next whole step, so a handle at the clip's end (which is rarely a whole second) moves onto whole seconds.
- **Dragging snaps** to the smallest of 1, 5, 10, 15, 30 or 60 s that is at least one pixel wide on the drawn track, so short clips get whole seconds and a 38-minute clip on a phone gets 10 s; within half a step of the clip's end the handle snaps to the end itself. A press on the track moves the nearer handle there (when both are equally near, the one on the pressed side) and focuses it, so the keyboard can take over.
- **Typed times are applied on leaving the field or on submit, and clamp with a message.** A time past the clip goes to the latest allowed; then the end gives way: a typed end becomes 30 s after the start when the part is shorter (or the end is before the start), or 15 min after it when longer; a typed start moves the end along when needed. Each adjustment says what it changed ("This part is 15 seconds. A part needs at least 30 seconds, so we set the end to 02:40."), in a polite live region the field points to. Text that is not a time is an error on the field and blocks the start. Submitting applies any typed time first; if one was unreadable or adjusted, the plan does not start and focus goes to that field, so the learner sees the change before committing to it. Text that still shows a handle's position changes nothing, so an end at 12:40.437 is not cut to 12:40.
- **Default.** The whole clip when it is 30 s to 15 min (FR-CI-4, as before). The part starts as 2:30 from the first speech (B04), moved back to end inside the clip; a clip of 2:30 or less is suggested whole. Until the learner moves a handle or types a time, the part follows that suggestion, so it moves to the first speech when the peaks arrive. A clip over 15 min offers "Whole clip" as `aria-disabled` with its reason (B04) and starts on the part.
- **First speech** is the first moment the peaks stay at or above 10 % of the file's scale for half a second (5 values in a row), minus a half-second lead-in, rounded down to the second; with no such moment it is 0. Room tone and hiss in a usable recording sit well under 10 %, and a click or a door shorter than half a second is ignored. The design's later refinement, snapping cuts to sentence ends once the transcript exists, needs the transcript and is not part of this story.
- **Hear start and Hear end** use one hidden `<audio preload="none">` on the clip's `media_url` (the 307 to a signed URL, ADR 0022), seek to 1.5 s before the cut, play, and pause 1.5 s after it (a timer from the moment playback starts, and a `timeupdate` check). Pressing either again restarts it; a playback failure is reported in an alert.
- **Data.** The page now reads the clip from `GET /contents/{id}` (`useContent`) instead of searching the library pages, which ADR 0023 left as a follow-up, because the picker needs `peaks_url` and `media_url`. The peaks are fetched once per URL (`usePeaks`, `staleTime: Infinity`); when they cannot be loaded the track is drawn without bars, the page says so, and the handles and fields still work.

## Consequences

- The server's checks do not change: `start_plan` already refuses passages outside 30 s to 15 min or past the clip (ADR 0023), and the `practice.sessions` CHECK already enforces 30 s to 15 min. The 30 s and 15 min limits are constants in the web client (`time.ts`) and the server; a configuration endpoint for them would let both follow one setting.
- The browser fetches the peaks JSON from storage after the API's redirect, so production storage needs a CORS rule allowing `GET` from the web origin (ADR 0020 only asks for `PUT`); the local SeaweedFS allows any origin. Playback through `<audio>` needs no CORS.
- Playwright cannot route a request that follows a redirect, so the end-to-end tests answer the peaks and the media at the API paths directly (with range support for the media).
- On a long clip the two handles can be only a few pixels apart; the nearer-handle rule, the keyboard and the typed fields keep both reachable.
- The 2:30 default and first-speech rule apply only to this page; Shadow segment picking is a separate story.
