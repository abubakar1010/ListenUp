# Voice recording consent

Version voice-2026-1, draft of 22 October 2026.

> **Draft, not yet legally reviewed.** This notice describes how ListenUp handles recordings. It has not been reviewed by a legal adviser and may change before ListenUp opens to the public. A changed notice gets a new version, and you will be asked again before your next recording.

In the Shadow step you speak along with the clip. To give you feedback, ListenUp records your voice. Please read this before your first recording.

## What is recorded

Your microphone, during each Shadow round, from the count-in to the end of the round. Each Shadow segment has three rounds, and each round is a separate recording.

## Why

To grade each round on speaking measures such as rhythm, pronunciation and fluency, to compare your three rounds, and to let you replay a round next to the original.

## Who processes it

Recordings are analysed by speech models on our own servers. A recording is sent to an outside service only if that service does not train on it, and such a service is named in the [Privacy Policy](/privacy) before it is used. Today none is.

## Never used for training

Your recordings are used only for your own practice. They are never sold or shared, and never used to train AI models, ours or anyone else's.

## How long it is kept

Each recording is deleted 90 days after you make it, or sooner if you delete the session, the clip or your account. The grades, feedback and word labels from a round stay with your session.

## How to delete or stop

- Delete a session or a clip to delete its recordings at once.
- Delete your account to delete all your recordings after the 7-day restore period.
- Withdraw this consent at any time in Settings. New Shadow rounds will then not record until you agree again. Recordings you already made stay until they are deleted as described above.

You can skip the Shadow step if you prefer not to be recorded.
