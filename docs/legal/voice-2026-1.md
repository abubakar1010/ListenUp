# Voice recording consent

Version voice-2026-1, draft updated 28 October 2026.

> **Draft, not yet legally reviewed.** DRAFT — pending the legal review in #102. This notice describes how ListenUp handles recordings. It has not been reviewed by a legal adviser and may change before ListenUp opens to the public. A changed notice gets a new version, and you will be asked again before your next recording.

In the Shadow step you speak along with the clip. ListenUp records your voice for private replay and planned speaking feedback. Automated Shadow grading is not enabled yet, so feedback is currently unavailable. Please read this before your first recording.

## What is recorded

Your microphone, during each Shadow round, from the count-in to the end of the round. Each Shadow segment has three rounds, and each round is a separate recording saved to your account. The microphone check before the rounds only measures sound locally: it does not save or upload audio.

## Why

To let you replay a round next to the original. Planned analysis will process your recordings with the reference transcript and original audio to provide speaking feedback and compare rounds; it is not enabled yet.

## Who processes it

Recordings are stored for private replay. Planned speech analysis processes recordings and reference transcripts on our own servers. No hosted speech service is approved under the current architecture. Any AI provider processing recordings, transcripts or gists must be bound not to train on them; an ineligible provider is not used. The [Privacy Policy](/privacy) describes processors and their outstanding details.

## Never used for training

Your recordings are used only for your own practice. They are never sold, published to other learners or used to train AI models, ours or anyone else's. Processing for your practice is described above.

## How long it is kept

The policy is deletion 90 days after recording, on request, or when the session or clip is deleted, whichever comes first. Grades and word labels stay after recording expiry. Account deletion has the separate 7-day grace period below. Deleting a clip deletes its recordings. Automatic expiry and deleting a single recording or session on request are not implemented yet (#90, #93); they must be ready before public launch. Recordings are currently stored without that automatic expiry.

## How to delete or stop

- Delete a clip in your library, after confirming, to delete the recordings made for it. Removal runs in the background and may take a short while; backup copies expire separately, as the Privacy Policy explains. Deleting a single recording or a single session are planned controls, currently unavailable. Their deletion policy is described above.
- Delete your account in Settings to disable it at once. Within 7 days, signing in lets you explicitly choose Restore; signing in alone does not restore it. Background purging removes the recordings after that grace period. Backup copies expire separately, as the Privacy Policy explains.
- Withdraw this consent at any time in Settings. New Shadow rounds will then not record until you agree again. Recordings you already made stay until they are deleted as described above.

You can skip the Shadow step if you prefer not to be recorded.
